## Claim

image-to-video clips is a concrete entry point for ai video workflow.

## Why it matters

Specific use cases reduce generic category copy and make the first workflow test easier to define.

## Evidence

- Audience: creators and operators who start from screenshots, still frames, artwork, or product images instead of filmed footage
- Trigger: A still image or screenshot set needs motion treatment, but the team does not yet know which workflow creates the cleanest first pass.
- Workflow: Choose the source image set, define the motion goal, compare one primary tool plus one fallback, then capture the settings that survive review.

## Counterpoint / limitation

A use case page gets weak when multiple jobs collapse into the same generic recommendation.
