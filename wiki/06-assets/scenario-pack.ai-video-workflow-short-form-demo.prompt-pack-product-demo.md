## Scenario

Turn one product release into a short-form demo clip

## Input

6 screenshots, one release note, one target channel, one CTA, and one reviewer from product marketing

## Expected output

A short production brief, 4-6 scene sequence, 3 CTA variants, and one reviewer risk note the operator can use before rendering the first pass

## Common failure

The first pass sounds generic, the scene order feels random, and the CTA lands too late to earn the next click

## Repair prompt

Rewrite the opening 3 seconds around the workflow outcome, move the CTA preview into the first half of the sequence, and rebuild the scene order to follow problem -> proof -> product step -> outcome -> CTA
