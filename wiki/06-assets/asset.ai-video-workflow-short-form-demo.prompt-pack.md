## Asset promise

A ready-to-run pack covering hooks, screenshots, transitions, and short-form demo prompts.

## Who it is for

indie hackers, product marketers, and content operators shipping short-form product/demo videos

## What the visitor receives

- Brief intake block: A one-screen intake for source asset, target channel, conversion goal, reviewer, and publish-ready definition before prompting begins.
- Variable prompt matrix: Prompt blocks for hooks, screenshot sequence, transitions, CTA framing, and variable placeholders that map directly to the first publish-ready short-form demo pass.
- Repair prompts: Fallback prompts for generic output, weak motion, unclear CTA framing, or sequence drift after the first pass.
- Review rubric: A compact QA rubric for clarity, motion quality, sequencing, and CTA placement before the clip leaves review.
- Reuse notes: A fill-in handoff note to capture what changed between launch one and launch two, including the winning angle, reviewer note, and failure point.
