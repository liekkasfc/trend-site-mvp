Core verdicts

Do not model AI video cost as one average price

The honest budget separates official tool pricing from estimates for failed retries, voice-over, editing, motion polish, review time, and outsourced labor.

Retries are part of the budget

A cheap tool path can become expensive when the team regenerates full clips because shot consistency, caption timing, or final edit quality keeps failing.

Provider quotes are required for labor-heavy work

Editing, voice-over, and motion graphics vary by scope, rights, format, revision count, and turnaround, so a quote is safer than a made-up market rate.
